Lab tools

CompressionWhere particles are squeezed close together. Higher pressure.
RarefactionWhere particles are spread far apart. Lower pressure.
WavelengthThe distance from one compression to the next (or crest to crest).
AmplitudeHow big the vibration is. Bigger amplitude = louder sound.
FrequencyHow many waves pass each second, measured in hertz (Hz). Sets the pitch.
MediumThe material sound travels through. Sound cannot travel through a vacuum.
